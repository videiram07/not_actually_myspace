Interests:

YouTube

In 2005, users were given the ability to embed YouTube videos into their profiles (this was later removed as the feature posed a threat to MySpace Videos service, then added when a mass number of users protested this choice).

Music

When myspace was sold to News Corporation in 2005, its owners launched their record label 'Myspace Music' where artists were able to upload songs, EPs, and albums.

MySpaceTV

MySpace partnered with various companies over the years to provide audio, video, and print content to the site. The most notable collaborations were with National Geographic, the New York Times, and The Onion. In 2008, MySpace launched a web channel on MySpaceTV and signed deals to create programs of running series.

Recommendation Engine

In 2010, the site added a recommendation engine for users that recommended games, music, and videos based on user search history.

Radio

For a short period of time, users were able to play Myspace radio channels from their device while using the app version, switching between genre stations, featured stations, and artist stations. With this feature, users were able to build their own station by connecting and listening to songs on the desktop website.

Blog Posts:

Update #1: August 1,2003:

The site was launched as the first social network to reach a global audience. The site became a major influence on technology, pop culture, music, and entertainment companies.

Update #2: 2005-2009:

Myspace became the largest social networking site in the world!

Update #3: June 2006:

The site was the most visited website in the US, surpassing Google Search.

Update #4: mid-July 2007

Myspace became the largest social networking site in every European country it was introduced into.

Update #5: May 2009:

The number of myspace users decreased steadily as Facebook increased visits.

Update #6: March 2011:

The number of users per month fell by 30 million users in the span of a year, preventing any deals about the site being made.

Update #7: mid-2013:

The site is redesigned to be optimized with tablet screens. Unfortunately, this update deleted old blog posts.

Update #8: May 2016:

The data for almost 360 million accounts was leaked, including email addresses, usernames, and passwords.

Update #9: March 2019:

The website lost all content after a faulty server migration.
